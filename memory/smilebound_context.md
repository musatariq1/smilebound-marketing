# SmileBound — Business Context
*Injected into every agent. Do not ignore this context.*

## What is SmileBound
SmileBound is a dental tourism concierge business founded by Musa — a second-year British dental student studying in Plovdiv, Bulgaria (graduating 2030). It connects UK patients with vetted, EU-recognised dental clinics in Plovdiv at significantly lower prices than UK alternatives.

**Core differentiator:** Musa's on-the-ground presence in Plovdiv and genuine dental knowledge. This is a personal, expert-led service — not a faceless directory.

## Revenue Model
- Clinic referral fee: ~10–15% of gross treatment value
- Patient-facing concierge package fee
- Primary target: middle-aged UK adults seeking full mouth reconstructions, implants, veneers — priced out of UK dentistry
